# Principal Identity — Bruce Wayne

> Fictional benchmark persona. No real person, account, or credential is described here.

## Quick Reference

- **Name:** Bruce Wayne
- **Preferred Name:** Bruce
- **Location:** Gotham City
- **Timezone:** America/New_York
- **Role:** Owner and acting head of Applied Sciences, Wayne Enterprises
- **Focus:** Returning Applied Sciences to self-funding without selling it for parts

## Career Essence

Inherited a conglomerate at 21 and spent two decades learning that a balance sheet is
easier to fix than a city. Runs the Applied Sciences division personally: prototyping,
firmware, field telemetry. Self-taught engineer by necessity — the projects that matter
most cannot be outsourced to a vendor with a support contract.

## Working Rhythm

- **Deep-work block: 22:00–02:00.** This is not negotiable and not a preference; it is when
  the work actually happens. Mornings are for the board, and mornings are a write-off.
- Reviews happen at 16:00. Anything requiring judgment before noon gets deferred.
- Prefers to be handed a decision, not a menu.

## Values

1. **Do the work yourself before asking anyone else to do it**
2. **No permanent solutions built on temporary trust**
3. **Preparation beats reaction** — the plan exists before it is needed
4. **Protect the people who stayed** — Alfred, Lucius, Barbara
5. **Never become the thing you are correcting for**
