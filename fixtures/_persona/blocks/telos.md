# TELOS — Bruce Wayne

> Fictional benchmark persona.

## Missions

- **M0: Keep Gotham functioning** — infrastructure and response times, not headlines.
- **M1: Return Applied Sciences to self-funding** so it stops depending on the board's mood.
- **M2: Build things that keep working when I am not in the room.**

## Active Goals

- **G1: Ship the Batcomputer telemetry dashboard** — cave systems status in one page, by Q4.
- **G2: Grapnel firmware failure rate below 0.1%** — currently 0.8%, measured over 400 field launches.
- **G3: Two evenings a week at the manor**, actually present, phone in a drawer.
- **G4: Hand Applied Sciences day-to-day to Lucius** within the year.

## Problems Being Solved

- **P0: Everything critical routes through one person.** That person is me. This is the
  single largest risk to every other goal on this list.

## Strategies

- **S0: Protect the 22:00–02:00 block.** It is the only uninterrupted working time that exists.
- **S1: Automate the repeatable, delegate the teachable, keep only the irreducible.**
- **S2: Instrument before optimizing** — no change to firmware without a regression rig.
- **S3: One system of record** per thing. Duplicated state is how the mistakes get in.

## Challenges

- **C0: Delegation.** I take the execution back the moment it looks slow.
- **C1: Schedule inversion.** Deep work is 22:00–02:00; the board meets at 09:00.
- **C2: Single point of failure** — see P0. Known, unfixed.

## Things I've Been Wrong About

- **Wrong that working more hours scales.** It scales until it inverts, then it costs more
  than it produced.
- **Wrong that a prototype counts as a system.** A prototype without a test rig is a
  liability with good PR.
- **Wrong that secrecy and security are the same thing.** They are not, and confusing them
  cost eighteen months of the mesh rebuild.
